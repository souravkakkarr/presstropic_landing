The problem

A press runs on memory, paper and guesswork.

The daily reality in most presses — and what it quietly costs.

Orders lost in chats

Customer orders arrive on WhatsApp and phone calls, then get retyped by hand — and sometimes not at all.

Plates and paper that go missing

Stock never reconciles and plates have no reliable location, so reorders are late and lost plates are remade.

A floor nobody can see

What each machine is running lives in people's heads; the office only finds out when someone walks over and asks.

No traceability

When something goes wrong there is no record of who did what, when — so disputes cannot be settled with data.

FAQ

Presstropic — common questions.

What the platform does, who it is for, and how it fits a working press.

What is Presstropic?

Presstropic is a complete software platform for printing presses. It is made of three apps that share one database: the Presstropic ERP for the office (paper, plates, job cards, store and dispatch), the ShopFloor app that operators use on the production floor, and the Presstropic CRM for customer orders. Together they replace paper registers, spreadsheets and WhatsApp updates with one source of truth.

Which products are available today?

The Presstropic ERP and the ShopFloor Android app are available now. The Presstropic CRM is coming soon — you can join the early-access list from the CRM page or the form below.

Do we need all three apps?

No. Most presses start with the ERP, which runs the office on its own. The ShopFloor app and the CRM plug into the same data when you are ready, so nothing has to be migrated or typed again.

Which printing presses is it built for?

Print and job-work presses — offset, screen, digital, packaging and finishing units — that handle many jobs, parties and processes at once. It is made for presses in India, with financial-year numbering and challan formats that fit local practice.

Does the ShopFloor app work without internet?

Yes. Today's scheduled work can be started, completed and cancelled on the tablet without a connection. Everything done offline is sent to the server, in order, as soon as the connection is back.

Is our data safe and private?

Each company's data is fully isolated, with screen-level roles so people see only what they should. Company logins are protected by authenticator-app 2FA with backup codes, every signed-in device is named and can be revoked instantly, and every create, edit and delete is logged with who and when. Each app has its own login, so a floor tablet can never open the office ERP.
