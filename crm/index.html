FAQ

Presstropic CRM — common questions.

What is coming, and how it fits the rest of the platform.

When will the CRM be available?

The Presstropic CRM is in early access. Join the list with the form below and we will reach out when it opens for your press.

What does the Order Request Form look like to customers?

It is a simple order page that shows your company's letterhead, on a permanent link that only changes if you choose to refresh it. Customers fill in what they need and submit — no account or app required.

Can the form sit on our own website?

Yes. You can embed the form on your own website, so your customers never need to see a Presstropic link.

How do orders reach production?

Every order lands in the Order Inward Register, where it is reviewed and turned into a job card in the ERP without being typed again.

Does the CRM have its own login?

Yes. Like every Presstropic app, the CRM signs in on its own, so the people who handle orders do not need access to the ERP.
